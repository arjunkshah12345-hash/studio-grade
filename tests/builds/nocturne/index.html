Stage 01 of 03Fri–Sun · 20:00–04:00

HAL

Peak-time techno under the crane rails.

The largest Fenix hall: steel trusses, a poured concrete floor, and the rig hung from the rails that once moved cargo to the quay. Four-to-the-floor from 20:00, the headliners after midnight, house lights at 04:00.

SILO

Live, strange, and in the round.

A circular grain store with speakers on every wall. Live sets, modular rigs, a choir, and the things without a genre yet. In the afternoon it becomes the listening room: seats, low light, one record at a time.

DOK

Bass, breaks, and a slow sunrise.

Getting there

Metro to Rijnhaven, then ten minutes on foot across Katendrecht. The water taxi stops at the quay. Bike parking at the north gate.

Hours

Nights 20:00–04:00, DOK until 06:00. Day programme Saturday and Sunday 13:00–18:00. Last entry 01:00.

Access

HAL and DOK are step-free; SILO has a lift. Quiet room, free earplugs and a companion ticket on request.

House rules

18+ with photo ID. Cashless bars. Phones welcome, flash photography is not. No re-entry after 02:00.
