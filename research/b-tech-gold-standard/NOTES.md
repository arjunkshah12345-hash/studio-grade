# B — Gold-standard tech product marketing sites (live client/brand work)

Researcher cluster: the marketing sites every startup copies. Evidence = live sites measured with `tools/analyze.mjs` at 1440×900 (+ 390w mobile) on 2026-10-06/07, screenshots viewed (intro frames, hero, scroll, mobile), plus credit lookups. All numbers below are measured computed styles unless marked "(visual)" = read off screenshots.

Raw data: `research/b-tech-gold-standard/sites/<slug>/data.json` + `shots/`. Compact digests: `python3 -I tools/btg-digest.py research/b-tech-gold-standard/sites/<slug>`.

Deliberately excluded (covered by the SaaS-studio researcher, do not duplicate): **Cursor** (site by basement.studio, Cursor Gothic type by Chimera), **ElevenLabs** (rebrand by basement.studio), **Granola** (2026 rebrand by Ragged Edge). ## Cross-site patterns (numeric ranges @1440w)

**Display headline size** (hero h1): 48 (Stripe) · 49 (Mercury) · 56 (Attio) · 61 (Anthropic) · 64 (Linear, Vercel, Raycast, Ramp) · 68 (Family) · 80 (Apple) · 96 (Resend serif). **Median 64px.** Hero h1 almost never exceeds 2 lines at desktop (exception Stripe's paragraph-headline, 4 lines). Nobody uses >100px except editorial subpages (Linear /method 128px serif) and Attio's 96px "Universal Context" band.

**Section h2**: 40–56px (Ramp 40, Linear/Attio/Stripe 48–56, Vercel/Resend 56, Apple 80 on chapter openers). Ratio h1:h2 is small (≈1.15–1.6) — the hero is not dramatically bigger than chapter headings; hierarchy comes from whitespace.

**Line-height on display**: 1.0 (Linear, Vercel, Ramp, Resend) to 1.1–1.15 (Raycast, Family, Anthropic, Stripe). Body 1.5–1.6.

**Tracking (display)**: sans display -0.015 to -0.03em is the norm (Linear -0.022, Stripe -0.02/-0.025, Family -0.02/-0.031, Attio -0.015/-0.024, Superhuman -0.027, OpenAI -0.03, Apple -0.015). **Outliers**: Vercel & Resend h2 **-0.05 to -0.06em** (deliberately compressed "engineered" look); Mercury & Raycast use **positive** tracking (+0.01–0.02em) for warmth/legibility. Small text: body tracking scales with size (Family -0.013@17 → -0.006@14; Apple -0.022@17, +0.007–0.011@21–28). Mono labels: +0.02 to +0.12em, uppercase.

**Body size**: 16–18px for marketing paragraphs; 13–15px for UI/feature lists (Linear 13–15, Vercel/Attio/OpenAI 14). Body color is never pure black/white: `#4D4D4D`, `#474645`, `#50617A`, `rgba(0,0,0,.85)`, `#8A8F98`, `#A1A4A5`.

**Weights**: display weights are mid, not bold — 300 (Stripe), 400 (Vercel, Ramp, Resend), 450–510 (Vercel h2, Linear, Superhuman, Mercury 480), 500 (Family, OpenAI, Attio), 600 (Raycast, Apple), 700 only Anthropic. Variable "in-between" weights (450, 460, 480, 510, 540) are a tell of custom/variable fonts tuned by eye.

**Font sourcing**: 10 of 16 use a custom or licensed-premium typeface (Söhne, Geist, arcadia, Anthropic Sans/Serif/Mono, Family, Lausanne, Domaine/Favorit, ABC Oracle/Exposure, OpenAI Sans, Super Sans, SF Pro, te-20). Inter appears only where the brand voice is "the product UI" (Linear, Raycast, Attio, Family body).

**Serif usage**: serif appears as a *second voice*: Anthropic (lead paragraphs), Attio (testimonial quotes, Tiempos), Linear (/method manifesto, Tiempos Headline), Resend (hero + finale, Domaine), Dia (all headlines, Oracle). Never serif for UI text.

**Mono usage**: 9/12 core sites have a mono family reserved for eyebrows, figure captions, stats, footnotes, spec labels (Berkeley Mono, Geist Mono, Commit Mono, Anthropic Mono, Favorit Mono, JetBrains Mono, SourceCodePro).

**Containers / margins**: content widths 1072 (Family) · 1204 (Raycast) · 1260 (Apple) · 1266 (Stripe) · 1280 (Linear, Resend) · 1284 (Anthropic) · 1312 (Ramp) · 1392 (Attio, inside rails) · 1448 (Vercel). Side margins: 24 (Vercel, Attio rails) · 64 (Ramp) · 78–80 (Linear, Anthropic) · 104 (Stripe, Resend) · ~184–208 (Family, Mercury).

**Section spacing**: explicit padTop 96 (Resend, Superhuman) · 120 (Family) · 128 (Linear) · 144 (Apple) · 224 (Raycast hero). Effective gap between chapters 160–300px (Vercel/Linear visually ~250px of empty canvas).

**Radius**: buttons — pill 9999 (Linear, Vercel hero, Mercury, Family, OpenAI, Dia, Apple) vs small-radius 4–8px (Stripe 4, Ramp 6–8, Vercel nav 6, Raycast 8, Anthropic 8, Attio 10, Resend 16, Superhuman 8–12). **Hairlines**: 1px at 5–12% alpha of the ink (`rgba(255,255,255,.08)`, `rgba(0,0,0,.08)`, `rgba(33,33,33,.1)`), often implemented as `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px` (Vercel, Family, Linear) to avoid layout shift and allow stacking.

## Signature techniques worth stealing (with implementation detail)

1. **Product UI as the hero illustration, rebuilt in HTML** (Linear, Attio, Ramp, Vercel). Not a PNG screenshot in a laptop mockup. Build a simplified but real-looking app window: sidebar 240px, 13px Inter 400/510, hairline `rgba(255,255,255,.08)` dividers, one live micro-state (agent "Thinking…" shimmer, a card tilted 4–6° mid-drag, a cursor). Frame: `border-radius:12px; box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.2), inset 0 0 0 .5px rgba(255,255,255,.08), 0 2px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.25)`. Let it bleed off the bottom of the hero (top edge at ~58% of viewport) and **mask-fade the right/bottom edges** to the page bg: `mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, #000 60%, transparent)`. Dim secondary panes to ~40% opacity so one focal element reads.
2. **Blur-in hero reveal** (Linear, Attio, Mercury). `from {opacity:0; filter:blur(10px); transform:translateY(8px)} to {opacity:1; filter:blur(0); transform:none}`, 800–1000ms, `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`, stagger 80–120ms: headline → subhead → CTA → product frame (+300ms).
3. **Two-tone statement headline**: `<h2>Statement sentence. <span style="color: var(--ink-60)">Elaboration that continues on the same line.</span></h2>` at 40–56px, same weight. Works dark (`#F7F8F8` + `#8A8F98`) and light (`#061B31` + `#50617A` / ink at 60%).
4. **Live-ticking stat eyebrow** (Stripe, Ramp): 12px label + tabular-nums value in a grey chip `background: rgba(0,0,0,.04); padding:2px 6px; border-radius:4px; font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, value increments every ~50–100ms with per-digit vertical roll (overflow:hidden digit columns, `transform: translateY(-n*1em)`, 300ms ease-out). 8 significant digits = precision theater.
5. **Layout rails** (Stripe, Attio): two full-height 1px lines at the content edges (`position:absolute; inset-block:0; left: var(--gutter); border-left:1px solid rgba(0,0,0,.06)`) plus horizontal 1px dividers between bands, occasional dashed lines `1px dashed` for diagram edges, and a 64–300px **accent segment** on a divider (Stripe's purple `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #4304EA 50%, transparent)` under the active stat/tab).
6. **Hairline bento via 1px grid gap**: `display:grid; gap:1px; background: var(--hairline);` children `background: var(--bg)` → perfect shared borders with no double lines (Attio 5/3/2-col cells).
7. **Top-edge glow line on dark panels** (Resend, Raycast): `.panel::before{content:"";position:absolute;top:-1px;left:10%;right:10%;height:1px;background:linear-gradient(90deg,transparent,rgba(160,130,255,.8),transparent)}` + gradient border via `background: linear-gradient(42deg,#141517,#191B1E) padding-box, linear-gradient(42deg, rgba(24,25,28,.88) 45%, rgba(215,239,248,.28)) border-box; border:1px solid transparent`.
8. **Shadow-ring borders** (Vercel, Family): `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.08)` (+ outer ring in bg color for separation). Stripe elevation for floating cards: `0 30px 45px -30px rgba(50,50,93,.25), 0 18px 36px -18px rgba(0,0,0,.1)`. Dia soft stack: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.04), 0 12px 28px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.1), 0 28px 56px -28px rgba(0,0,0,.12)`.
9. **Skeuomorphic key/button** (Raycast): 4-layer shadow (above) on `#1B1C1E` keycaps 8px radius; glow state = `box-shadow: 0 0 40px 8px rgba(255,99,99,.35)` + red label. Use for any hardware-ish metaphor.
10. **Fused email capture capsule** (Mercury, Ramp): one rounded container (`radius 12 / 999`, 1px border, light fill) with input left and the primary button *inside* the right end (4px inset). Converts better and looks bespoke vs. input+button side by side.
11. **Sticky persistent CTA** (Dia bottom dome, Ramp ticker bar, Apple local-nav Buy): Dia's dome = fixed bottom element `width: 140vw; left:-20vw; height: 220px; border-radius: 50% 50% 0 0 / 100% 100% 0 0; background:#FBFAF6; box-shadow: 0 -7px 18px -8px rgba(73,54,34,.09)` with a 440×56 black pill centered.
12. **Scroll-spy chapter list** (Attio, Dia): left column sticky list of 3–5 chapter titles, active = ink + 2px left bar, inactive = 25–42% ink, mono numerals `01/02/03`; right column swaps visuals with crossfade 300–400ms.
13. **Hero media card → full-bleed on scroll** (Anthropic, Apple): rounded 1284px card with documentary video; ScrollTrigger scrub scales `clip-path: inset(0 78px round 24px)` → `inset(0 0 round 0)` over ~1 viewport.
14. **Gradient-filled display type** (Apple, Resend): `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#E4F6F0,#9DCFCA 31%,#6B95AC 68%,#45657D); -webkit-background-clip:text; color:transparent` — subtle, cool, low-saturation; never rainbow.
15. **Proof-by-customer-site chapters** (Vercel): headline + grayscale screenshot of a real customer's site + one quantified proof sentence + 4-item feature list. Grayscale keeps the page monochrome while showing third-party brands.
16. **Engineering captions**: "FIG 0.1" mono labels above isometric line drawings with dotted hidden edges (Linear); spec-table footers on cards with mono uppercase keys and right-aligned values (Anthropic). Makes content feel documented, not decorated.
17. **Single-weight type system** (Ramp Lausanne 400 only; Teenage Engineering 100/300): hierarchy purely by size, opacity and space — instantly reads "designed".
18. **Logos**: monochrome (white on dark / ink on light), 7 across, evenly spaced across full container, one row, optional slow marquee; captioned by a mono micro-label ("POWERING THE COMPANIES BUILDING THE FUTURE") or a quantified line ("Join 70,000 of the world's most ambitious companies…").
19. **Testimonial walls**: two rows of cards moving in opposite directions with edge fades (`mask-image: linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #000 10%, #000 90%, transparent)`), card = logo tile + name/title + 14–16px quote (Ramp, Resend). Or one huge serif quote (Attio Tiempos 48px; Linear 32px on light cards).
20. **Grain + light**: Raycast's WebGL bars carry visible film grain; Dia uses defocused natural light video; Mercury uses cinematic dawn landscapes; Anthropic uses macro analog-instrument film. Texture comes from *light and lens*, not from noise overlays on flat colors.

## What these sites never do (conspicuously absent vs typical AI-generated landing pages)

- **No purple-to-blue gradient blobs behind the hero text**, no glassmorphism cards floating on gradients (Stripe's ribbon is a single art-directed shader, not decoration). 
- **No emoji in headlines, no "🚀 Supercharge your workflow"** copy; no exclamation marks in headlines. Headlines are 3–8 words, declarative, often punny or category-defining.
- **No three-feature-cards-with-icons row** directly under the hero as the main content. Features are told as chapters with real UI, not icon+title+2-line card grids.
- **No stock photography or generic 3D blobs/abstract spheres**; the few 3D objects are tactile and specific (Resend cube, Mercury clay toggles, Raycast keycaps).
- **No ALL-CAPS headings** (uppercase share 0–5% except Ramp 20% micro labels and Rive's game-UI outlier). Caps are reserved for ≤12px mono labels.
- **No heavy 700–900 display weights** (only Anthropic 700, Rive 900 outlier); no text-shadows; no outlined/stroked display text.
- **No centered-everything layouts**: heroes are left-aligned in 6/12 core sites; centered heroes (Raycast, Family, Attio, Dia) compensate with strong imagery.
- **No smooth-scroll hijacking libraries** (0 Lenis/Locomotive); no scroll-jacked pinning marathons except Apple's product chapters.
- **No "Trusted by" logos in full color**, no star ratings badges, no "As seen on" bars with mismatched logos (Mercury's press cards are uniformly tinted).
- **No more than 2 hero CTAs**; secondary CTA is ghost/text, never a second loud color.
- **No pure #000 text on pure #FFF backgrounds for body** (Vercel `#171717/#4D4D4D` on `#FAFAFA`; Dia `rgba(0,0,0,.85)` on `#FBFAF6`; Anthropic `#141413` on `#FAF9F5`). Dark sites avoid pure white text (`#F7F8F8`, `#EDEDF3`, `#F0F0F0`).
- **No generic Inter-at-700 with -0.02em everywhere**: weights are tuned (450/480/510) or the font is custom.
- **No bouncy/elastic overshoot on page-level reveals** — springs are critically damped (Family damping 80) or eases are expo-out; overshoot appears only on tiny UI (Linear `(0.45,1.45,0.8,1)` on a toggle-scale element, Apple chips).
- **No footer-less pages** and no tiny footers: all end with a confident one-line finale CTA ("Built for the future. Available today.", "Take the short way.", "Email reimagined. Available today.", "Ready for a better day?") then a 4–6 column mega footer.

## Rankings — best 4 references for an agent to emulate

1. **Linear (linear.app)** — the most transferable system: Inter at tuned weights, -0.022em display, two-tone h2s, `#08090A` palette with 8% hairlines, 128px section rhythm, product UI rebuilt in HTML as the illustration, blur-in reveal, mono figure captions. Every element is reproducible with CSS; no heavy assets needed.
2. **Stripe (stripe.com)** — best light-mode enterprise reference: light-weight (300) display, navy/slate two-tone, live stat eyebrow, layout rails, the canonical layered shadow, optically corrected 4px-radius buttons, bento of stylized UI tiles, accordion case studies. Teaches density without clutter.
3. **Vercel (vercel.com)** — masterclass in restraint and monochrome: one logo as hero art, -0.06em Geist display, shadow-ring borders, customer-proof chapters in grayscale, huge whitespace. The best antidote to "AI-slop" decoration.
4. **Ramp (ramp.com, Red Antler + Bakken & Bæck)** — best for "alive" marketing pages: single-weight Lausanne, lime highlighter accent, fused email capture, ticking metrics + sticky agents ticker, bento cards with two-tone titles and ↗ affordance, before/after "Systems that never spoke" storytelling, punny confident copy.

Honorable mentions: **Anthropic** (warm editorial: sans/serif/mono trio, documentary film, spec tables), **Dia** (brand atmosphere via natural-light video + persistent dome CTA), **Apple** (one-sentence-per-screen pacing, gradient type, frosted price capsule), **Raycast** (physical metaphors on dark).
