# Halden 3 (direction 6 Cinematic Place, Confident): skill feedback
Final audit: 0 fail · 1 warn (layout.length 6.7 vh vs 12–27) · 10 pass

1. Audit false positive, `a11y.contrast`: `stackBg` returns null when the sample point (rect centre) is below the viewport, then falls back to `effBg` (DOM ancestor background). Frost text over the kitchen photo was scored against the page mist: 1.05:1. Skip the element and re-check on a later pass rather than fall back.
2. §44 (card → full bleed) with copy set on the photo: put the clip-path on the frame, not the img, and give the frame the photo's dominant colour. The copy then never sits on the page ground mid-animation or before a lazy image loads. This also fixed (1). Worth a line in §44.
3. Mona Sans draws a slashed zero in tabular figures. `.t-num` on stats, prices and the clock gave "2,4ØØ" and "ØØ:14". typography.md should say: check the zero under tnum, and use proportional figures for display numerals.
4. tokens.css bundles use the `font:` shorthand, which resets `font-stretch`. A wide-cut system (stretch 125%) needs a later `font-stretch` rule on every bundle. Expose `--display-stretch` / `--label-stretch` in the bundles.
5. Hero recipe that worked first time, worth adding to direction 6: anchor the 21:9 photo to the hero's bottom, mask its top 0→34%, and use the sky's sampled mean as the page ground, so the h1 straddles the seam. Set the hero to 100svh − nav − 60px, or a masthead that crosses the bottom edge falls below the fold at 1440×900.
6. The direction says booking is never more than zero scrolls away, but it gives no calendar recipe, and halden1 already banned native date inputs. Add one to components.md: a range picker, past dates off, closed season shown in the motif (here misted months with a plain note), a close button for the mobile bottom sheet, and a result line that states the "from" price and says plainly that nothing is booked.
7. The [data-theme] trap, a new variant: a block that is dark on desktop (copy over a photo) and light on mobile (copy above it) has to re-declare its ink at the breakpoint. A chip coloured `var(--paper)` inside it then went dark on dark. Suggest fixed hex pairs for chips, or a scope that flips both tokens.
8. layout.length for direction 6 (12–27 vh) can't be met by a brief with four facts unless you invent content. Suggest the audit say "brief-limited: judge" when there are no placeholders to fill, or lower the floor for single-property hospitality.
9. Bible note: "left third clean wall" (the room shot) came back as a mid-grey wall in shadow (L≈0.22). Ink there passes only at display size (4.6:1). When type will sit on a wall, ask for a "pale, lit wall".
10. Port trap: `python3 -m http.server` on a busy port dies quietly in the background, and the first audit ran against another build (Morrow). The printed page title caught it. The prompt should say: curl the title before auditing.
