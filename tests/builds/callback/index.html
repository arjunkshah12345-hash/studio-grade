Fair questions.

It sounds like a receptionist. If a caller asks, it tells them plainly that it’s an AI assistant answering for your business.

What counts as an emergency?

Jobs like a burst pipe or no heating. For those it doesn’t book a slot: it calls your on-call person straight away and passes on the caller’s details.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward the number you already have to Callback. Setting it up takes about 15 minutes.

When does it answer?

Day and night, in under two rings, including evenings and weekends.

What if I go over 300 calls in a month?

Every call after the 300th that month is $0.60. There’s no second plan to move up to.
