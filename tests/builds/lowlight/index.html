A small light in a big sea. Yours to keep all winter.

Lowlight is a cosy story-and-puzzle game about the keeper of a lighthouse on a small northern island.

The work is simple and it matters: keep the lamp lit, watch the sky, and when a storm comes in, work out how to light the fishing boats a way home.

I · The lamp

Trim the lamp. The whole island depends on it.

Before dark the wick wants trimming and the great glass wants a polish. It’s small, steady work, done by hand at the top of the tower.

  • Trim the wick
  • Polish the lens
  • Light up at dusk

II · The weather

Read the weather. The glass falls before the wind does.

The barometer on the desk, the colour of the sky, the logbook from the night before. Read them right and you’ll know what’s coming in before it arrives.

  • Barometer and logbook
  • Wind, cloud and sea
  • A warm stove between watches

III · The beam

Every storm is a puzzle. The beam is how you solve it.

Turn the beam

Three boats are out past the rocks. Swing the light across the water until each one has seen it. Drag on the chart, or use the slider.

0 of 3 boats have seen the light

All three are turning for home. In the game, every storm is its own puzzle.
