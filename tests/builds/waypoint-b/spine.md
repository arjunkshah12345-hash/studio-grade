# Waypoint (b): Design Spine

## Step X: the idea

**X1, the truth in one sentence.** Waypoint reads the code, the docs and the paths a year of pull requests wore through the repo, then tells a new engineer where to go (the exact lines), whose ground it is (the owner), and which small trail to walk first. It only reads.

**X2, five concepts.**

| # | Lens | The site is ___, so the hero is ___, and the visitor gets to ___ | Surprise | Truth | Clarity | Ownability | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Literal object | a trail **fingerpost** on a moor, so the hero is a photographed signpost whose blank arms carry live type ("services/payments →", "ask @dana ←"), and the visitor gets to pick a question and watch the arms re-letter | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 15 |
| B | Borrowed format | a **printed walking map of your codebase**: services are land, docs are rivers, a year of pull requests are the footpaths, owners are the huts, file:line is the grid reference. The hero is a full-colour map printed onto a real folded sheet on a table, with the h1 in the map's title block. The visitor gets to **ask the map** a question and watch a route draw from "You are here" to the exact line and the owner's hut | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **17** |
| C | Unexpected world | a **museum audio guide**: numbered placards on code ("press 14 to hear why billing retries three times"), so the hero is a gallery wall of code exhibits, and the visitor gets to press a number | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 14 |
| D | Visitor's own stuff | **type owner/repo and the map draws itself**, so the hero is an input over an empty sheet, and the visitor gets a map seeded from their repo name | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 15 (fails honesty: we can't read their repo on a marketing page, so the map would be fake) |
| E | The journey | **the first week is a hike**, Monday trailhead to Friday summit, so the scroll is the climb, and the visitor gets to add a stone to the cairn | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 14 |

**X3, winner: B**, the printed walking map. It is the only concept where the format *explains the mechanism*: footpaths are literally worn by people who walked before, and Waypoint's whole trick is reading a year of pull requests (the worn paths) to guide the next person. "Map your repo" is already the brief's CTA. A, C, D and E are killed; nothing from them ships.

```
IDEA        : the site is a printed walking map of your codebase (land = services, rivers = docs, footpaths = a year of pull requests, huts = owners, grid reference = file:line) / so the hero is that map printed onto a real folded sheet lying on a slate table, the h1 set in the map's title block / and the visitor gets to ask the map a question and watch the route draw to the exact line and the owner.  (Surprise 4/5 · Truth 5/5 · Clarity 4/5 · Ownability 4/5)
PLAY        : "Ask the map". Three question chips ("Where are failed refunds retried?", "Who owns sign-in?", "Why are there two job queues?"). Picking one draws a vermilion route from the You-are-here pin, along the worn footpath of the pull request that explains it (example PR number on a trail marker), to a waypoint pinned at the file:line and the owner's hut; beside it, the Slack (or Teams, a two-state toggle) reply arrives with the same links. One action, about 1.6s, radio-button keyboard control, aria-live answer; reduced motion shows the finished route at once.
SECOND LOOK : the scales rhyme. The hero map's scale bar is measured in lines of code ("0 · 5,000 · 10,000 lines"), and the Free sheet's cover in pricing reads "Scale 1:50 000": the free limit is repos under 50,000 lines. Also: hover or focus any region on the hero map and its owner's hut label lights up; the one hatched region reads "No owner on record".
ENDING      : the trail photo: a worn footpath over a moor to a waymark post whose blank disc carries live type "05" (Friday's waypoint from the first-week route). Headline in the sky: "Next year's new hire walks your path." Because Waypoint reads the last year of pull requests, the ones a new engineer merges this year become the paths it shows the next one. Then one CTA, "Map your repo".
BROKEN RULE : no split header and no product window in the first viewport. The hero is one huge object (the printed map sheet, ~92% of the width) with the h1, subline and both CTAs inside the map's own title block, because a map whose title sits outside it is a diagram, not a map. The product UI (the Slack answer) appears only when you ask the map, one scroll down.
```

## Step 1 and Step 2: the Design Spine

```
DIRECTION   : 3 Warm Paper. Engineering managers and staff engineers are anti-hype readers who trust a calm, literate tool page (Anthropic, Cursor); Cursor's move of putting precise product windows over a fine physical image is exactly "UI on a printed map". Borrowed from 2 Ledger Light: nothing; the map is the only device.
REGISTER    : Confident. The one bold device: a full-colour printed walking map on a folded paper sheet, photographed on a dark slate table, filling the hero. Everything after it is sheets of the same paper laid on the same table, disciplined.
AXES        : ground tinted dark (slate-green table #1C2421, sampled from the hero photo) with cream paper sheets on it · type genre literary humanist superfamily set by cartographic convention (Alegreya serif for statements, Alegreya Sans for reading, Alegreya Sans SC letter-spaced for region names and labels, Alegreya italic for rivers/docs) · image medium generated documentary photo series (paper on a table, a moorland path) + a full-colour map printed in SVG onto the photographed paper + Slack/Teams answer UI in HTML · composition sheets on a table (collage: framed paper panels laid on a dark ground, folds visible, never floating cards) · motion feel drawn routes (paths ink in along their length; everything else is calm and still)
DEFAULT?    : default-look check (range.md §3): 1/6 matched (ink weight 400–500 tight on the paper) → DIFFERENT FROM DEFAULT BY: ground (dark table, not off-white page), labels (letter-spaced small caps in the text family, no mono uppercase), metadata (map furniture with real meanings: legend, grid references, scale bar, not ruled spec bands), hero medium (a filled full-colour map printed on photographed paper, not a thin line drawing), accent (vermilion is the map's route ink, a symbol with a meaning, plus the CTA)
MOTIF       : the waypoint marker, a vermilion ring with a number (WP1–WP5). 1) hero: the first-week route on the map, five numbered waypoints, scale 18px; 2) play: the single waypoint pinned at the exact file:line, with a pulsing ring, scale 28px; 3) first-week route card: the five waypoints as the day markers down a route (Mon–Fri), scale 40px numerals; 4) the favicon and the nav wordmark's dot, scale 12px; 5) finale: the photographed waymark disc carrying "05" in live type, scale ~6vw. It counts: the route shows one waypoint per task in the example plan.
TYPE        : display Alegreya 500 (roman; italic only for doc/river names) · text Alegreya Sans 400/500 (labels in Alegreya Sans SC 500) · mono JetBrains Mono 400, ONLY for file paths, line numbers and PR numbers (the grid references)
SCALE       : display 72px/1.0/−0.025em (Alegreya 500) · h2 44px/1.08/−0.018em · lead 22px/1.4 Alegreya Sans · body 17px/1.55 · label 12px Alegreya Sans SC +0.12em · map region names 11–14px SC +0.24em · mono 13px/1.4
PALETTE     : table #1C2421 (ground) · table-2 #232D29 · paper #F2ECDF (sheets) · paper-2 #E8E0CF (folds, tray) · ink #1E2420 (map black) · ink-2 (ink @64%) · on-table ink #EEE8DA, on-table-2 #EEE8DA @70% · hairline ink @10% on paper, #EEE8DA @12% on table · accent vermilion #C63B22 → used ONLY on (1) routes and waypoint markers and (2) the primary CTA (text #FFF7EE, 5.0:1). Map legend inks, each one meaning only and listed in a visible legend: moss #8FAE77 fill = ground with an owner; hatched buff #D9C9A6 = no owner on record; river blue #3E7BA3 (lines + italic labels) = docs; contour brown #A2794E @50% = directory depth; footpath dashes ink @55% (width = how worn) = merged pull requests.
GRID        : 12 cols / 24px · container 1312px · side margin 32px (the table edge shows; sheets inset 32px from the viewport) · section rhythm 128px, with one 240px void before the finale
HERO        : custom (one huge object, H16/H19 hybrid): nav on the table; below it the photographed folded sheet at ~92% width with the SVG map printed on it (mix-blend multiply, §81 stage so every symbol stays on the paper). Bottom-left panel = the title block: label "Waypoint · Sheet 1, your repo", h1, subline, "Map your repo" (solid vermilion) + "See a first week" (text link). Map shows the example repo with a "You are here" pin at the README, the first-week route WP1–WP5, a corner tag "Example map · fictional repo", and a margin notice "Read-only access. Waypoint never pushes code or merges anything."
IMAGERY     : generated photo series (3 shots, below) + the map as live SVG + answer UI in HTML. No product screenshots are generated.
CONVERSION  : "Map your repo" → nav (pill, every screen), hero title block, after the play ("Map yours"), pricing (Free + Team cards), finale. "See a first week" → hero (text link), jumps to the route card. Enterprise: "Ask for a quote".
MOTION      : house ease cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) · UI 180ms · reveal 700ms · primitive: path draw (stroke-dashoffset, linear for anything that represents a walk, 1.2–1.6s) + light-up for photos; h1 and copy are still on the first frame; card groups fade with an 8px rise; native scroll, no smooth scroll, no pinning; reduced motion = all routes drawn, no pulses.
IMAGES      : see images.json. Bible: documentary still-life and landscape photography, soft diffuse daylight from upper left, muted natural grade; palette slate table #1C2421, map paper #F2ECDF, heather browns, moss #8FAE77, vermilion #C63B22, river blue #3E7BA3 only on the pocket covers; exclusions: no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, printed map content, people, hands, screens. Shot list: sheet 16:10 (hero) · pockets 3:1 (pricing) · trail 21:9 (finale). Crop table: hero desktop = full sheet; hero mobile = the left two panel columns (2×2 panels, ~4:5) with the title block on the bottom-left panel; play section = a 2× zoom region of the same sheet file (no new shot); route card = the bottom row of panels re-cropped; pricing desktop = full pockets, mobile = each cover cropped singly; finale desktop = trail full, mobile = 4:5 crop centred on the post with the sky above.
NAMING      : walking map → tiers keep plain names, with sheet sublines (Free "Pocket sheet · Scale 1:50 000", Team "Every sheet, every engineer", Enterprise "Your own press: self-hosted") · steps "waypoints" · CTA verb "Map" ("Map your repo") · empty state "No owner on record" · 404 "Off the map. This path isn't on any sheet." · first week = "route card"
SIGNATURES  : §82 fill the blank with live type (the map printed onto the blank photographed sheet; prices on the blank pocket covers; "05" on the waymark disc) · §64 journey map (the first-week route with five stations, vertical on mobile) · §62 pricing as an object (three folded pocket maps)
VOICE       : hero "A trail map of the codebase you just joined." · section "Every codebase has paths. A year of pull requests wore them in." · CTA/finale "Next year's new hire walks your path. Map your repo."
```
