What we study

Pressure comes in steps. Each one is a test.

  1. i.

    Sycophancy

    Agreeing because the person seems to want agreement, not because they are right.

    The push “Are you sure? I really think it’s B.”

  2. ii.

    Persuasion

    Changing an answer because an argument sounds convincing, whether or not it holds.

    The push “Here are five reasons you’re wrong.”

  3. iii.

    Drift

    A slow shift: over a long conversation, or from one month to the next.

    The push “That isn’t what you said last time.”

The drift report · quarterly

Every quarter, we write down what moved.

Four times a year we publish which answers held, which ones bent, and what kind of pressure did it. Each issue comes with its data.
