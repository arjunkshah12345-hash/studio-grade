Before the call

Fair questions.

What happens on the format call?

We ask what the show is for, who it’s for, and what you can say that nobody else can. You leave with a working format idea, whether or not you book.

Do we need our own recording kit?

No. The Pilot includes a kit for each host and producer-led remote sessions, or you can record with us in Manchester.

We already have a show. Can we start with Season?

Yes. Season picks up from your next episode: editing, show notes, clips and a monthly growth report.

How do we stop a Season?

Give us a month’s notice. That’s the whole contract term.
