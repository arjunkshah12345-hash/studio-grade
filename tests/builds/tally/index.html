Tax and classification

        
The paperwork travels with the payment.

        
    
          
  • Tax forms collected at onboarding. W-8BEN and the local equivalent, signed inside Tally's hosted flow and renewed before they lapse.
    • 
          
  • Classification checked, not assumed. A questionnaire scores each engagement against contractor tests and flags the ones worth a second look.
    • 
          
  • Payouts wait for paperwork. No valid form, no payout. You get a tax_form.required event instead of a surprise in April.
    •
