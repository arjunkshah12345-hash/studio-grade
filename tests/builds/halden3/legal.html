Terms and privacy

Halden is not a real hotel. This site is a design study. There are no rooms, no sauna and no kitchen to book, and the photographs are generated images of a place that does not exist.

Terms

Nothing on this site is an offer. The booking form does not reserve, hold or charge anything, and the prices it shows are worked out from the example rate of NOK 2,400 a night.

Privacy

The site sets no cookies and sends the dates you pick nowhere: they stay in your browser and are gone when you close the page. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.
