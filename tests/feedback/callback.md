# Callback (gallery v5) · skill feedback

1. Direction 11 + H19 + §81 stage worked first time: the 21:9 hero with a reserved cabinet zone took ink type with no scrim, and the ring ripple stayed locked to the face-down phone at 1440 and 390. Say in §68 that a stage is worth it even for one small overlay.
2. The default-look heuristic counts transcript and FAQ rows as `hairlineBands` (≥14 borders). A chat transcript with a rule between every line tipped it to 4/6. Either exempt `details`/`ol` inside product replicas or note in range.md §3 that transcripts should separate lines by spacing, not rules (that fix was also the better design).
3. `mobile.overflow` came from a flex waveform: 64 bars × (2px min-width + 3px gap) = 320px of min-content. Worth a line in components.md: give decorative flex bar rows `min-width:1px`, gap 2px and `overflow:hidden`.
4. Logo check (components.md) caught a real problem: a dot plus two quarter-arcs read as the RSS icon. Add "signal arcs = RSS / Wi-Fi" to the list of marks that collapse at 16px.
5. A §69 tray on a photo needs the step copy *inside* white cards when cards cross the photo's edge, or the copy lands on the photo. Spell that out in §69/§77 (image-beside variant only covers the tray case).
6. The bookend crop: re-using the hero file at a different object-position still read as "the hero again". A separate `export --crop` that makes a different subject the focus (here the phone) solved it. Suggest §68 says "re-crop to a different focal point", not just "re-crop".
7. With no customers, the strongest proof slots were: a labelled example call per trade (plumbing / electrical / heating), the AI-disclosure line inside the sample transcript, and the §83 pricing dots (filled = included, open ring = $0.60). The motif doubled as the pricing legend. A good worked example for "When the brief lacks a slot".
8. A visible audio placeholder works best as a disabled dashed play button + "Placeholder" chip + the full transcript open below. The transcript carries the section, so the placeholder never sits on screen alone (imagery.md §5 discipline applies to audio too).
9. The audit's motion profile lists tokens.css eases (.16,1,.3,1 / pop / inout) even when the page only uses one house ease, which makes the "one house ease" check noisy. Count only eases on elements with non-zero transitions in the profile line.
10. Direction 11 is a good fit for small-business audiences when the bible is documentary photography rather than illustration. A note that 11 can be "plain and warm" (one sturdy grotesk, sentence-case labels, no serif) would help builders who think 11 means a book serif.
