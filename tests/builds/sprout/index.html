Fair questions.

What if it gets my plant wrong?

Tap "Not this one" and Sprout shows the closest matches, or search all 9,400 species by name. Your plant keeps its name and face either way.

Why does it want to know which way my window faces?

Light is what most people get wrong. Sprout combines the window direction with your local forecast to work out how much light each plant actually gets, and tells you when to move it.

What happens when I add a sixth plant?

Your first five stay free. Adding a sixth asks if you'd like Sprout Plus at $3.99 a month. Say no and nothing changes.

How does vacation mode work?

Set your dates and pick a friend from your contacts. While you're away, Sprout texts them when a plant needs water: which one, where it is, and how much. They don't need the app.

iPhone or Android?

Both. Same app, same faces.
