# Copy: voice, formulas, bans

Award juries give 10% of the score to Content, and it rises when the copy has a voice. Every top site has at least one line you could only say about that brand.

## 1. Headline formulas (with measured examples)

Hero headlines run **2–8 words** (median 4). They are declarative, specific, and rarely end in "!".

| Formula | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Category claim** (own the noun) | "The product development system for teams and agents" (Linear) · "Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue." (Stripe) · "The intelligent documentation platform" (Mintlify) · "Inference is everything" (Baseten) |
| **Twist / pun on an idiom** | "Time is money. Save both." (Ramp) · "Take the short way." (Raycast) · "We've got the receipts." (Ramp) · "Subtle. Power." (Oura) |
| **Contrarian / reframe** | "The computer, de-invented" (Daylight) · "Radically different banking" (Mercury) · "Design was never meant to happen through buttons" (illoca) |
| **Imperative** | "Control every pixel." (BFL) · "Make your software improve itself" (Factory) · "Engineer Without Limits™" (ICOMAT) · "Stop editing. Keep shooting." (Elva) |
| **Two-beat** (statement. answer.) | "Invasions move fast. Be faster." (Inversa) · "Better outcomes. Built on Sierra." · "Scouts always come back." |
| **Place / mission** | "A campus that brings change home" (Obama) · "Spatial design at eye level" (Studio D) · "We design the places where people love to be together." (Populous) |
| **Question as the product** | "What can I help with?" (OpenAI) · "What's your yard costing you?" (Terminal) |
| **Mechanism in the headline** | "Convert any file with a right-click." (convt) · "40 formats, one menu." (convt) · "Buy it once, or go Pro." (convt) · "Stop uploading your files to convert them." (convt finale: name the enemy) |
| **One word** | "Image" (BFL) · "ANTARCTICA" (White Desert) · "Now Open" (Lucas) |

Section heads are **two-tone sentences**: a statement + elaboration ("A new species of product tool. Purpose-built for…"). Finale lines: "Built for the future. Available today." · "Email reimagined. Available today." · "Ready for a better day?" · "Be the first on the line." · "What hasn't been can now be."

## 2. Voice registers

- **Systems / engineering** (Linear, Factory, Vercel): low-adjective, nouns, verbs. "Plan, build, review, and ship software with autonomous agents across desktop, terminal, web, and CLI." Feature labels are nouns ("Intake and integrations").
- **Literary / wry** (Aesop, JW Anderson, Kinfolk): "Consider bathroom embarrassment, banished." · "Newsletters that smell nice." · "To discover the most suitable formulations for your bathroom performances…" Em dashes are welcome.
- **Humane / contrarian** (Daylight, Anthropic): "We refuse to accept a future where our devices are exhausting, addictive, and distracting."
- **Civic / imperative** (Obama, AIC): "We need your help to turn hope into action." · "Admission is always free."
- **Laconic luxury** (Totême, Aman): place names as headlines ("Amanvari, Mexico"), two-word captions ("The New Shearling"), "Discover more".
- **Wry tech** (Serve, Bennett & Clive): "Why move a 2-pound burrito in a 2-ton car?" · "THOUGHTFUL. PRECISE. THE OPPOSITE OF CHAOTIC."

Write 3 sample lines in the Design Spine before building. If a line could appear on a competitor's site unchanged, rewrite it.

## 3. CTAs: name the task

Good: "Download for Mac" · "Start building" · "Talk to an engineer" · "Plan your visit" · "Explore the collection" · "Book appointment" · "Reserve" · "Configure now" · "Create your mission" · "Read the story" · "See all hours" · "Watch film ▶".
Acceptable when paired with context: "Get started", "Learn more →".
Bad: "Get Started Today!" · "Unlock the Power" · "Join the Revolution" · "Click Here" · "Submit" · "Shop now!!".

## 4. Proof copy

- **Precise numbers beat round ones:** "99.999%", "$1.9T", "1.72802563%", "Pages read 10,759,862". Footnote claims ("86%…¹").
- **Name names:** customers, quotes with roles, and real logos instead of "Trusted by 10,000+ teams".
- Quantified customer proof per chapter: "Notion powers millions of agent conversations daily on Vercel."

## 5. Microcopy (where craft shows)

- Stock and shipping: "✓ IN STOCK · SHIPS IN 3-5 BUSINESS DAYS". Read time: "Five-minute read". Status: "All systems normal", "Open today 10–5".
- Sold out: strike-through on the size; "Large to size" fit notes.
- Success: "You're on the list! Cheers. You will not regret it."
- Errors: human and specific, never "An error occurred."
- Empty states, 404, and cookies are on-brand and short.
- Sound prompts: "For an optimal experience, please turn on your sound."

## 6. Banned phrases (each one is an AI or template tell; the audit script flags most)

Supercharge · Unleash · Unlock (the power of) · Revolutionize · Elevate (your) · Empower · Transform your… · Seamless(ly) · Effortless(ly) *(OK only when literally true and specific)* · Next-gen / next-level · Cutting-edge · State-of-the-art · Game-changer · Harness · Leverage · Robust · Synergy · Best-in-class · World-class · Look no further · In today's fast-paced world · Take X to the next level · Whether you're a… or a… · Say goodbye to… *(Daylight earns it once; you probably don't)* · AI-powered *(as a headline adjective)* · Lorem ipsum · "Feature 1/2/3" · emoji in headings or buttons · "!" in headlines · "🚀".

## 7. Mechanics

- Sentence case for headings (except a deliberate all-caps brand stance).
- Use the Oxford comma consistently. Em dashes without spaces (or spaced en dashes, but consistently). Curly quotes.
- Numbers: tabular in tables, thin spaces or commas consistently, currency formatted for the locale (Totême: "5 600 $").
- Every paragraph earns its place. Hero subline ≤ 2 lines (≤ 25 words). Section intros ≤ 3 lines.
