# Range: register, axes, and escaping the default look

The rest of this skill is measured from medians, and **medians produce the average site**. Followed literally, the rules converge on one look. That look is tasteful, but it is the same every time: warm off-white paper, near-black ink, mono uppercase labels, hairline metadata bands, a thin line drawing as the hero, and one tiny accent. The award winners in the research do not converge. Lando Norris, MindMarket, Norton, Obama, The Drake, Seventeen, and Family are loud, colourful, and still top-tier. This file is how you get range without losing discipline.

**The core distinction:** discipline is about *count* (one idea, one type system, one colour logic). It is not about *volume*. A site can be extremely loud and perfectly disciplined: MindMarket is one typeface, one weight, and a lime field the size of the viewport.

---

## 1. Register: pick the volume before the details

Choose one register in Step 1, next to the direction. Each direction can be played at any register; the register changes the defaults below. **When the register and the direction recipe disagree, the register wins** (a Loud Institutional site uses directions.md for structure, voice and content rules, and range.md for ground, type and colour).

| Register | What changes | Measured exemplars |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiet** | Off-white or white ground. One accent under 5% of pixels. Light or mid weights. Space does the work. | Totême, Aesop, Steve Jobs Archive, Anthropic, Cursor, Studio D, Vercel |
| **Confident** | One bold device at scale: a coloured band, a highlighter accent, a big product stage, a dark hero. The rest stays disciplined. | Stripe (blurple + ribbon), Ramp (lime highlighter `#E4F222`), AlphaSense (electric-blue split band `#0040FF` + acid yellow), Mercury (cinematic photo), Oura, Linear, Resend |
| **Loud** | Colour as the **ground**, not the accent. Type as poster (130–320px, condensed or heavy allowed). Illustration or colour blocks as the image. Motion with personality (springs, pops). | MindMarket (full-bleed `#8ED462`, Inter 500 at 140px −0.06em, character crowd), Lando Norris (`#282C20` olive-black + `#D2FF00`, mixed-font caps at 105–110px), Norton (full-bleed `#6098FF` / `#F6BE11` / `#76A93D` sections, 75% uppercase), Obama (230px condensed "DONATE TODAY" on `#0064F0`, yellow alert band `#FFBD12`), The Drake (word-chip headlines, burgundy `#672B3C` and lavender `#B5B7D8` bands, marker scribbles), Seventeen (a totem of colour bars as the hero), Family (a character cast, one display-p3 hue per feature), Ace Hotel (140px condensed caps over film, orange BOOK NOW) |

The ceiling for loud is Agent Arcade: five accents, gradient headline text, glow borders. **Loud is not many colours; loud is one colour logic played big.**

What stays fixed in every register: ≤3 families (Loud/Playful may run a 4th only when every face has exactly one written job, techniques.md §66), one motif, one colour *logic* (not necessarily one hue), contrast ≥ AA, ≤2 hero CTAs, a voice in the copy, and real content as the image.

Loud-register rules:
- Colour may cover 30–100% of a viewport as a **field**. Use one hue per field, and set the text in a dark or light tint *of the same hue* (Dropbox Brand: `#684505` on `#FAD24B`), or in pure ink if it passes AA.
- A multi-hue system is fine when **each hue is assigned to a meaning**: a section, a product line, a category (Norton per section, Family per feature, Fondation Cartier per exhibition, Seventeen per project).
- Display type may go heavy (600–900) **if** it is condensed, a brand display cut, or a deliberate poster register (Obama, Lando, Ace). Bold + wide + neutral grotesk is still template-y.
- Motion can show personality: springs with overshoot on small elements, pops, wiggles, morphing blobs as *section transitions* (MindMarket), and characters that react.

## 2. The five axes: choose deliberately, not by default

Every site is a point on these axes. The skill's defaults sit at the first option of each. **Pick each axis on purpose and write it in the Spine.**

### A. Ground (the colour of the page)
| Ground | When | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Warm off-white paper | quiet, literary, luxury (**overused by this skill's outputs: justify it**) | `#FAF9F5` Anthropic, `#F7F7F4` Cursor, `#FFFEF2` Aesop |
| Pure white | fashion that lets the product speak; crisp engineering | Totême, Akris, Vercel `#FAFAFA` |
| Tinted dark | dev tools, night, cinema, luxury tech | `#08090A` Linear, `#282C20` Lando olive, `#052424` Terminal teal, `#020A18` Hubtown navy, `#100904` Oryzo brown |
| **Mid-tone** | earthy, warm, grown-up, unexpected | `#7C6758` Cartier taupe, `#6E6548` Pasqua olive-khaki, `#A0A5B1` Igloo grey, `#AFAEA7` Seventeen warm grey, `#B3ABA3` Vast taupe |
| **Saturated field** | playful, civic, festival, youth, confident consumer | `#8ED462` MindMarket, `#6098FF` / `#F6BE11` Norton, `#0064F0` Obama, `#0040FF` AlphaSense, `#D2FF00` Lando, `#0B0F5E` Miu Miu ultramarine, `#EBFC72` Inversa lime |

| Hairline / light giant grotesk | editorial, calm, luxury | Exo Ape Lausanne 300 at 250px/0.73, Studio D PolySans 300 at 128px/0.66 |
| **Condensed gothic caps** | civic, sport, poster, hospitality, protest | Obama Gotham Condensed 100–230px/0.8–0.9, Ace Toronto Gothic 140px, White Desert Oswald 256–320px, Ayocin Systemia 240px |
| **Wide / extended** | architecture, fashion, real estate, auto | ERA Maison Neue Extended, Scout wide cut |
| **Didone / high-contrast serif** | fashion, wine, luxury, drama | Symphony of Vines Palace 150px caps −0.03em, ERA serif + script |
| Light literary serif | warmth, books, wellbeing | Daylight Arizona Flare 300 −0.07em, Oura Editorial New 300 −0.05em |
| **Soft / rounded** | consumer, kids, food, friendly finance | Family custom rounded, Choo-Choo Fredoka |

Mono uppercase labels are a **device of directions 1, 2, and 8** (dev, SaaS, deep-tech) and of the editorial metadata layer. In other directions, label with the text face: small caps, sentence case, or italic serif.

### C. Image medium (what fills visual space)
| Medium | Notes | Seen on |
|---|---|---|

| Photography / film | when assets exist | Mercury, Sierra, Populous, Aesop |
| Technical line drawing + mono labels | deep-tech, hardware (**overused by this skill's outputs: one site per set at most**) | Baseten isometrics, Oryzo CAD loader |
| **Illustration cast** | consumer, community, civic, B2B with warmth | Family blob creatures, MindMarket rubber-hose crowd, Teenage Engineering comics, Obama commissioned illustrators |
| **Colour-block composition** | brands, agencies, systems | Seventeen colour-bar totem, Dropbox tiles, Norton fields |
| **Type poster / kinetic type** | culture, festivals, sport, launches | Obama rotating noun, Bennett & Clive name stack, Drake word chips, Miu Miu letter-spread block |
| **Object in CSS/SVG/3D** | a single hero product; tactile | Resend chrome cube, Raycast keycaps, Mercury clay toggles, Choo-Choo toy UI |
| **Collage / cut paper / archive** | heritage, research, editorial, food | Scout fanned archive cards over display type, Granola cut-and-paste findings, Daylight cards over headline |
| **Pattern system from the logo** | institutions, rebrands | Obama rising-sun arcs in colour tiles |
| Data as art | finance, climate, sport | Ayocin 170px numerals, Stripe ticking stat, Inversa GIS map |
| Dither / halftone / ASCII | docs, retro-tech, file and media tools | Mintlify dither, convt halftone stage, Baseten glyphs |

### D. Composition archetype
Product stage (UI on an art layer) · **Poster** (one giant statement per viewport) · **Index** (the list is the hero: Bennett & Clive, Pentagram) · **Banded** (full-bleed colour bands per section: Norton, Drake) · **Collage** (overlap, rotation, cut-outs: Scout, Daylight testimonials) · Editorial asymmetric grid (Studio D) · Spec sheet (rules, tables, coordinates; the skill's default, **overused**) · Small image, big room (Totême).

### E. Motion feel
Calm resolve (blur-in, ink-in; the default) · **Springy and characterful** (Family springs, MindMarket blob morphs, Choo-Choo pops and wiggles) · **Cinematic scrub** (pinned sequences, Apple, Radian lights-up) · **Kinetic type** (rotating words, letter-spread builds, marquees at 200px) · Instrumental (tickers, decode, counters).

## 3. The default-look check (run it on your Spine)

The **Studio Grade default look** is this combination:
1. a warm off-white ground (L 93–97, slightly warm);
2. a near-black ink at weight 300–500, tight tracking;
3. mono uppercase tracked labels as the metadata layer;
4. hairline-ruled bands carrying coordinates, batch numbers, times or specs;
5. a thin SVG line drawing (chart, diagram, product elevation) as the hero image;
6. one tiny accent used on marks only.

**If your Spine matches 4 or more of these, change at least 2**, unless the brief truly demands them (a deep-tech spec sheet, say). Write "DIFFERENT FROM DEFAULT BY:" in the Spine with the axes you changed. The audit flags the default look (`style.defaultLook`).

**In a set** (several sites in one project, a portfolio, a gallery, variants for a client), **no two sites may share more than 2 of the 5 axis values**. Change the ground, the type genre, *and* the image medium between them.

## 4. Confident done right: worked recipes

Most SaaS, fintech and product briefs land here: **one bold device at full scale, everything else disciplined.**

**The colour band (Stripe, AlphaSense, Tally)**
- One saturated hue as a **full-bleed band** that is the hero stage (or one key section), 70–110vh tall. Not a pill, not a glow.
- Text on the band: the band's own hue pushed to near-white for primary (`#F2F4FF` on cobalt `#1D3CF2`) and a lighter tint for secondary (`#CBD5FF`). Check both against AA; never 50% white.
- **The product crosses the band edge**: the dashboard or UI sits half on the band and half on the white below (`margin-bottom: -18vh` on the stage, the UI `translateY(50%)`). That crossing is what makes it read as designed, not as a coloured header.
- Below the band: white or near-white, ink type, the band hue reused only for numbers, links and the primary button.
- An art layer inside the band (a dot-map, glyph field or texture) at 8–15% contrast.

**The single object at scale (Raycast keys, Resend cube, Mercury landscape)**
One tactile object or one cinematic image fills the first viewport; type is small and calm around it. The rest of the page is Quiet.

**The big number (Ramp, Stripe GDP ticker, Ayocin)**
One live or precise number at 120–200px as the hero subject, with the claim beside it in body type.

**Mid-tone grounds** (chipboard, taupe, olive-khaki, L ≈ 0.2–0.45): the 74 / 62% ink tiers fall below AA here. Compress them to **~88 / 80%** of a near-black ink (or flip to a near-white ink if L < 0.25), and build hierarchy from **size, weight and a second face** (an italic serif) rather than opacity. Check every tier against the actual ground.

## 5. Loud done right: worked recipes

**Saturated field + character cast (MindMarket / Family)**
```css
:root{--ground:#8ED462;--ink:#1E2A12;--c2:#F5E211;--c3:#FF705D;--c4:#2BA0FF;
  --display:clamp(4rem,1rem + 9vw,9rem);--display-wt:500;--display-ls:-.06em;--display-lh:.92}
.hero{background:var(--ground);color:var(--ink)}               /* the field IS the hero */
```
A flat SVG cast (4–8 characters, limited palette from the system), entering from the bottom on springs with overshoot. Section changes are blob morphs (`clip-path: path()` or SVG), and cards are white with 24–64px radii.

**Civic poster (Obama / Norton)**
A black hero with a condensed caps rotating noun at 100px/0.9. Each section is a full-bleed colour from a 3-colour system, with text in a same-hue dark partner (`#18368A` on `#6098FF`). 0px radius buttons, 58–74px tall. The finale ask sits at 16vw condensed caps on the action colour.

**Hospitality with personality (The Drake / Ace)**
Word-chip headlines (each word in a bordered white box), burgundy and lavender bands, a hand-drawn marker underline on one word per section, mono-caps metadata, and the booking widget in the header.

**Sport / personality (Lando)**
A tinted-dark ground (olive-black), one neon accent taken from the subject (the helmet), mixed-font caps headlines (one word in a heavy display face), a collectible gallery, `elastic.out` only on small pops.

**Illustrated launch world (Ignura)**
A warm paper ground with landscape fields (sky blue, grass green, sun yellow) and one fire-orange brand colour for the CTA and the handwritten emphasis word. A bespoke cast with faces maps 1:1 to services. Sections divide on horizons and torn-paper bands. Headlines are a Fraunces-style serif at 600–650 with one Caveat-style handwritten word. Prices sit in a pixel face on ticket objects. Motion: characters idle (blink, hop) on finite loops, small pops spring, and big surfaces ease out.

**Agency index (Seventeen / Bennett & Clive)**
No display type: the hero is a colour-bar totem or a 150px client-name stack, with video behind the active item and a `mix-blend-mode: difference` header.

## 6. Range checklist

- [ ] Register chosen and written in the Spine (Quiet / Confident / Loud), with the reason.
- [ ] Ground, type genre, image medium, composition, and motion feel each chosen deliberately.
- [ ] Default-look check run; at least 2 axes changed if it matched 4 or more.
- [ ] In a set: no two sites share more than 2 axis values.
- [ ] Loud register still has one colour *logic*, ≤3 families, and AA contrast.
