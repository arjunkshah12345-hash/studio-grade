05:40

The harbour, before the bakeries open

Cold water slapping stone, a coil of wet hemp rope, and from a side street the first rye loaves going into the oven. Salt first; the warmth arrives late.

Opening
Sea air, cold stone
Heart
Hemp rope, rye crust
Base
Ambrette seed, toasted malt

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK

08:15

Bicycles over wet cobbles

Rush hour on the bridge: rain drying off the stones, a cardamom bun eaten one-handed, a wool scarf that has been out in it. Brisk and green, softer by nine.

Opening
Green cardamom, petrichor
Heart
Black coffee, wet wool
Base
Cashmeran, oat

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK

12:00

A reading room facing the water

Flat white light on long tables. Paper, cedar pencil shavings, the warm dust of a radiator that has been on since October. The quietest of the six.

Opening
Aldehydes, pencil cedar
Heart
Paper, orris
Base
Radiator dust, white musk

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK

16:50

Low sun across pine boards

The sun drops under the roofline and lands on an oiled pine floor. Black tea gone cold in the cup, a little tar from the boats on the canal, and the heat that stays in wood after the light has gone.

Opening
Black tea, bitter orange
Heart
Scots pine, linseed
Base
Birch tar, labdanum

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK

19:30

Dinner with the windows open

Smoke from a grill two courtyards over, sea buckthorn on the table, juniper in the glass, and the first cold air of the evening coming in over the sill.

Opening
Sea buckthorn, blackcurrant leaf
Heart
Juniper berry, rhubarb
Base
Beechwood smoke, tonka

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK

23:10

The last swim off the quay

Cold harbour water drying on warm skin, the lime trees along the quay, a bar somewhere with its door propped open. It sits close and lasts until morning.

Opening
Linden blossom, bergamot
Heart
Mineral accord, skin musk
Base
Vetiver, oak cask

Eau de parfum, 50 ml 1,450 DKK
