# SuperCompress "Pressed" (direction 11, Confident): skill feedback

1. images.py `paper` samples the four corners. On one-ink plates with a deckle (cyanotype, engravings on a mount) the corners are the paper, not the ground, so it can't unify a dark ground. Add `--ground '#0E396C' --box x,y,w,h|auto` (sample the darkest ~30% of a region). Also, its one-point multiplicative LUT tints the whites when the target is darker than the source. I wrote a two-point map (0→0, ground→ink, paper→paper, 255→255); it belongs in images.py.
2. Add `images.py cutout --key luma` for white-on-dark media. Luminance alpha plus "lighten" compositing (so one specimen never darkens another) plus dropping components under 3% of the largest let me derive the missing footer plate (§76 skyline) from the three plates I had. The current cutout assumes white sheets. Document "derive missing shots by re-laying cut-outs" in imagery.md §8 as the fallback when the image quota runs out.
3. Add a §76/§72 move: **deckled print edges as section edges.** A strip cut from a plate's own brushed edge, masked into the band texture, makes every blue field start and end like a print. It replaced straight band edges and was the biggest finish gain after the hero.
4. imagery.md: "sample the type-zone colour as the ground" isn't enough for textured media. A flat hex beside a textured plate reads as a different blue. Recommend a mirrored 2×2 tile of the clean zone as the ground texture. Using the hero itself as a `background-size:300%` texture exposed its deckle mid-band, and the screenshots caught it.
5. Whole prints shown on the page paper need a ≤1% edge feather. A 1.4–1.8% feather blurred the deckle; 0% showed the image rectangle's seam.
6. tokens.css trap, hit again: overriding `--ink-rgb` in a scoped section doesn't re-derive `--ink`/`--hairline`. Directions with field bands (11 Confident) should get a ready `[data-field]` scope with the derived tokens redeclared.
7. Audit false positive, `a11y.names`: links inside a closed `<details>` have empty innerText, so FAQ answers containing links get flagged. Skip `details:not([open])` descendants.
8. Audit false positive, `copy.hype`: a brand page's "we don't write" list (in `<del>`) gets flagged. Skip `del`/`s` text, or treat it as INFO.
9. Bug class worth a components.md line: `white-space:pre` on an `<ol>` of code lines renders the newlines between `<li>`s as blank lines (doubled the vignette height). Put `pre` on the `li`.
10. Font-relative max-width on a wrapper (`max-width:15em` on .head while the h2 inside has its own size) squeezed the h2 to a 3-word column. Say "put measure on the heading itself, in em of its own size" in layout.md.
11. Mobile H19: the "text card on the sampled ground above a portrait crop with a top mask" recipe worked first time. Crop the mobile file inside the deckle (export --crop), or a ragged edge shows on only two sides of the card.
12. Logo process: a 16px sheet (16/24/32/64/128, light and dark) killed four candidates fast. Side-view press marks read as hamburger menus and plain leaf tiles read as eco apps. Suggest "render a size sheet before choosing a mark" in the brand guidance.
13. The honesty layer ("What these numbers don't say", numbered, in a paper card on the blue band) read as the strongest proof section. Worth adding to §11 as the pattern for benchmark-led dev tools.
