Terms and privacy

Leave the Lights is a fictional campaign, made as a design study. Brekkøy, its council and its streets do not exist, and the photographs on the campaign page are generated illustrations, not pictures of a real place.

Terms

This page is provided as-is for demonstration. Nothing on it is a real petition, and signing the letter does not send it anywhere: the sending endpoint is a placeholder.

Privacy

The letter form runs entirely in your browser. Your name, neighbourhood and email are not stored, sent or shared. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts; it sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
