01 · Heat & cold

A sauna on the water. The ladder goes straight down into the fjord.

It is small, wood-fired and close enough to the fjord that hot and cold are a few rungs apart.

  1. 01

    Heat

    Sit by the stove until you have had enough.

  2. 02

    Cold

    Down the ladder and into the fjord. Breathe out slowly.

  3. 03

    Air

    Back up to the deck. Stand in the mist for a minute.

  4. 04

    Again

    Back inside, and round as many times as you like.
